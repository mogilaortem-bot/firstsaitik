# How Did Ancient Humans Choose Where to Sleep?

Thumbnail: **SLEEP HERE?**

## Description

Before bedrooms, mattresses, and alarm clocks, people still had to answer a very practical question: where are we sleeping tonight?

Follow one fictional traveller as we explore brush huts at Ohalo II, ancient plant bedding at Border Cave and Sibudu, and what present-day sleep studies can—and cannot—tell us about sleeping near other people. Different places, different periods, and a surprisingly familiar problem hiding underneath the grass.

The cartoon camps and buildings are illustrative reconstructions. The ancient sites discussed here are separated by large distances and spans of time. Modern communities are not direct stand-ins for prehistoric people.
