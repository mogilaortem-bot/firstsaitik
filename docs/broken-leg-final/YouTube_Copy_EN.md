# YouTube publishing copy — final episode

Title: You Broke a Leg 30,000 Years Ago. Now What?

One bad step. A crack. No hospital.

Would a broken leg have ended your life in the Stone Age? Follow an imaginary traveller from a sudden fall to the long problem of recovery, then examine real archaeological clues from Shanidar Cave and Liang Tebo in Borneo.

In this Ancient Bergman episode, the mystery goes beyond surviving an injury: what can healed bones tell us about care, and how do researchers distinguish a severe accident from a possible operation?

The traveller and camp scenes are illustrative fiction. Archaeological illustrations are not exact reconstructions, and bone diagrams are schematic. The Borneo amputation interpretation is presented with the published challenge and the original researchers' reply. The modern healing comparison is explicitly identified as modern.
